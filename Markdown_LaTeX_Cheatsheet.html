Writing maths in Markdown — one-page cheat sheet

SCIE1500. The same notation works in SciQuant Assistant, in GitHub and GitLab, in Jupyter notebook Markdown cells, and in most Markdown editors that support LaTeX maths.

The two things to remember
Inside a line: $ ... $ → the area is $A = \pi r^2$ here.
On its own line: $$ ... $$ → puts the formula centred on its own line.
Money trap: a bare $ starts maths. For dollars, write \$10, not $10.

Several lines, matrices, cases

Working shown line by line (align on the =):

$$\begin{aligned}
y &= mx + b \\
  &= 2x + 1
\end{aligned}$$

A piecewise function:

$$\begin{cases}
x & x \ge 0 \\
-x & x < 0
\end{cases}$$

A matrix: pmatrix ( ), bmatrix [ ], vmatrix | | — same pattern, & between entries and \\ between rows.

If something doesn't appear
  • Check your $ signs come in pairs, and that you meant maths — a stray $ swallows the rest of the line.
  • Stick to \begin{aligned} for multi-line working. It is the one that works everywhere. Some tools also accept \begin{gather} and \begin{align}, but not all of them do, so your working may look fine in one place and vanish in another.
  • Keep links and images out of the maths — \href and \includegraphics are not widely supported. Write them as ordinary Markdown outside the $ ... $.
  • Everything on this sheet is deliberately in the common subset, so it renders the same wherever you paste it.
  • Stuck on a symbol you can't name? Draw it at detexify.kirelabs.org and it tells you the command.
